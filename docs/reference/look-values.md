# Look values

This page lists the measured values of the island's look: lighting and shadows, exposure, water
and albedo. Design quantities (a size, a height or an angle chosen for the look) stay on the design
page, beside the decision they belong to. What each value means for the look, and why, is in
[Art direction](../design/art-direction.md) and the subject pages it lists.

## Albedo

Linear diffuse albedo of the island's main surfaces.

| Surface | Value |
| --- | --- |
| The meadow's blades | about 0.08, the albedo of a sunlit leaf |
| The cliff's stone | about 0.1, darker than real limestone (0.2 to 0.35) on purpose |
| The soil between the blades and under the groves | about 0.09, a yellow-brown earth, with grain from a scanned ground |
| The bamboo's culms | about 0.05 averaged over a grove, where most culms are old. A new culm: about 0.07, a warm olive, with red about three quarters of green. An old culm: about two thirds as light, and browner. A dull brown mottle about as dark as an old culm covers up to about a third of an old culm's base. Albedo varies 0.85 to 1.15 times from culm to culm |
| The bamboo's leaves | about 0.12 averaged over the crowns, with red about 0.6 of green and blue about a quarter of green. Each plant lies on a band from a dull grey green at about 0.03, through a clear saturated green at about 0.12, to a pale yellow green at about 0.13; 12 % of the plants lie in the band's darker half |
